🏆 ATP PE Tournament

Organize ladder tournaments where players challenge higher-ranked opponents to gain points and improve their rank.

🤺 Challenge Rules
  • Players can only challenge opponents ranked above them.
  • Maximum rank gap allowed is 5 ranks.
💡 Example: If ranked 8th, you can challenge players ranked 7, 6, 5, 4, or 3.
🔢 Points System

Points earned depend on the rank gap between the players:

Opponent +5 ranks5.5 pts
Opponent +4 ranks5 pts
Opponent +3 ranks4.5 pts
Opponent +2 ranks4 pts
Opponent +1 rank3.5 pts
Opponent -1 rank3 pts
Opponent -2 ranks2.5 pts
Opponent -3 ranks2 pts
Opponent -4 ranks1.5 pts
Opponent -5 ranks1 pt
ⓘ In case of defeat, the losing player always receives 0.5 points.
📊 Ranking
  • Standings update automatically after match confirmation.
  • Players are ranked in descending order of total points.
  • Rank #1 corresponds to the student with the most points.
