Guide & Rules 使用指南与规则
📖 How to Use This App 快速使用指南
1. Setup & Roster 班级准备

Click Class Rosters 班级名单 on the home screen. Paste your student roster from Excel/CSV (one name per line) and tap "Import Names 导入名单". Tap "Launch Tournament 开启比赛" to initialize the ladder.

点击首页“班级名单”，直接复制Excel或ManageBac中的学生姓名粘贴并点击“导入名单”，点击“开启比赛”即可开始。

2. Choose Your Sport 选定运动项目

Swipe the horizontal carousel on the home screen to pick your active sport (e.g., Badminton, Table Tennis, Pickleball). The titles and score exports will match your selection.

在首页横向滑动图标，选择羽毛球、乒乓球或匹克球等当前正在进行的项目。

3. Challenges & Logging Matches 发起挑战与记录比分

Students find an opponent ranked up to 5 spots above them. Tap Match WITH Referee (有裁判) or WITHOUT Referee (无裁判), select the players, and confirm the score.

学生只能向上挑战最多高自己5名的对手。在场地边选定一名学生裁判，点击下方按钮依次选定裁判、获胜方和负方，确认即可实时更新积分。

4. Podiums & Gradebook Export 领奖台与成绩导出

Tap 📊 on the standings screen to view real-time awards (Most Matches, Victories, Climbers, and Top Referees). In Settings (⚙️), export the complete gradebook spreadsheet to CSV with one tap.

点击排位看板右上角“📊”可浏览四项荣誉领奖台；在设置(⚙️)中可将完整积分、胜率及裁判次数一键导出为CSV表格。

🏆 ATP Ladder System 梯级挑战赛机制

Inspired by professional ATP tennis ladders, this format eliminates bracket downtime. Instead of sitting out after a loss, all students stay continuously active, challenging nearby opponents to climb the class rankings.

源自ATP网球积分排位赛机制，彻底解决传统淘汰赛淘汰即下场坐等的问题。全班学生全程参与、随时挑战相邻对手以争夺更高梯级。

🤺 Challenge Rules 挑战规则
  • Upward Challenges 向上挑战: You can only challenge peers ranked above you on the leaderboard. (只能挑战排名高于自己的同学)。
  • 5-Rank Gap Limit 5级差距限制: Maximum allowed ranking gap is 5 ranks (最多只能挑战高出自己5个位次的对手)。
  • Defender Duty 防守义务: Defenders must accept legal challenges to protect their ladder position (被挑战者必须应战以保住排位)。
💡 Example 举例: If currently ranked #8, you may challenge #7, #6, #5, #4, or #3. (若当前排名第8，可挑战第3至第7名)。

📢 Student Officiating 学生裁判制度

Resting students are encouraged to officiate courts. Every match logged with a student referee awards an immediate bonus (default: +0.5 pts, customizable by teacher).

轮空休息的学生可以作为主裁判主持邻近场地。执裁每次均可获得0.5分加分，培养规则意识与领导力。

🥇 Podiums & Collective Honors 领奖台与多维荣誉

The app honors achievement beyond raw match wins across 4 categories (综合表彰四个维度的优秀表现):

  • Most Matches Played 最活跃选手: Rewards grit, physical activity, and participation (比赛场次最多)。
  • Most Victories 胜利之星: Rewards top match winning performance (获胜总场次最多)。
  • Best Progression 最佳进步奖: Celebrates students who climbed the furthest up the ladder (排位位次提升最多)。
  • Top Student Referee 最佳金哨裁判: Honors student officials who contributed the most arbitrations (执裁场次最多)。
🔒 Teacher PIN Code 教师安全验证码

A customizable 4-digit PIN ensures student referees cannot accidentally enter invalid scores, override 5-rank limits without permission, or clear the database.

可自定义4位教师PIN码，用于特批跨级挑战（差距大于5级）、手动修改积分及撤销比赛比分。
