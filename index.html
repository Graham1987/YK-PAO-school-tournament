🏆 ATP PE Tournament

Organize dynamic ladder tournaments where players challenge higher-ranked opponents to win points and climb the standings.

🤺 Challenge Rules
  • Players can only challenge opponents ranked above them.
  • Maximum allowed rank gap is 5 ranks.
🔢 Points System

Points earned depend on the rank gap between the players:

Opponent +5 ranks5.5 pts
Opponent +4 ranks5.0 pts
Opponent +3 ranks4.5 pts
Opponent +2 ranks4.0 pts
Opponent +1 rank3.5 pts
Opponent -1 rank3.0 pts
Opponent -2 ranks2.5 pts
Opponent -3 ranks2.0 pts
Opponent -4 ranks1.5 pts
Opponent -5 ranks1.0 pt
ⓘ In case of defeat, the losing player always receives 0.5 points.
